# Fictional Company Travel Policy

This document is a synthetic example for a software portfolio project, not a real employer policy.

## Hotels
For domestic business travel, the standard hotel reimbursement cap is GBP 180 per night including taxes. Exceptions require written manager approval before booking.

## Meals
Employees may claim up to GBP 45 per day for meals while travelling overnight on approved business. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Rail
Standard-class rail tickets are reimbursable. First-class rail travel requires advance written approval from the travel operations team.

## Flights
Economy class is the default for flights shorter than six hours. Premium cabins require prior approval from the travel operations team.

## Receipts
Itemised receipts must be submitted within 14 calendar days after travel concludes.
